Privacy Policy for ResearchMate

Last Updated: January 2025

Privacy First: ResearchMate stores ALL data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on remote servers. Your highlights, notes, and tags never leave your computer.

1. INTRODUCTION

This privacy policy explains how ResearchMate ("we", "our", "the extension") handles your information. ResearchMate is designed with privacy as a core principle - all your data stays on your device.

2. DATA STORAGE

ResearchMate stores data exclusively on your local device using Chrome's built-in Storage API. Specifically:

  • Text highlights you save from webpages
  • Tags and notes you add to highlights
  • Source URLs and page titles
  • Your settings and preferences (theme, font size, etc.)

Important: All this data is stored locally in your browser. We do NOT:

  • Upload your data to any servers
  • Sync your data to the cloud
  • Have access to your saved content
  • Store any data remotely

3. NO ACCOUNT OR AUTHENTICATION

ResearchMate does not require:

  • Creating an account
  • Providing an email address
  • Signing in or authentication
  • Any personal information

You can start using ResearchMate immediately after installation with complete anonymity.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Your data is used solely to:

  • Display your saved highlights in the extension
  • Enable search and organization features
  • Generate citations from your saved sources
  • Export your data when you choose to

Since all data stays on your device, we have no access to it and cannot use it for any purpose.

5. DATA BACKUP AND EXPORT

ResearchMate provides export functionality to help you backup your data:

  • Export to plain text (.txt)
  • Export to JSON (.json)
  • Export to Markdown (.md)

These exports are saved to your local computer. You control where and how to store these backups.

6. DATA DELETION

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual items anytime from within the extension
  • Clear all data by uninstalling the extension
  • Manually clear extension data via Chrome settings: chrome://settings/content/all

Since data is stored locally, uninstalling the extension permanently deletes all your saved content.

7. CHROME PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

ResearchMate requests these Chrome permissions for specific purposes:

  • storage - Store your highlights, tags, notes, and settings locally on your device
  • activeTab - Read selected text from the current webpage only when you click "Save to ResearchMate"
  • contextMenus - Add the "Save to ResearchMate" option to your right-click menu
  • scripting - Extract selected text and page metadata (title, URL) when you save a highlight
  • Access to all websites (<all_urls>) - Allow you to save highlights from any website you visit

Important: We only access webpages when you actively save content. We do not:

  • Track your browsing history
  • Monitor which sites you visit
  • Read page content unless you explicitly save text
  • Run scripts in the background on pages

8. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

ResearchMate does NOT use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking:

  • No Google Analytics or similar tracking
  • No crash reporting services
  • No advertising networks
  • No cloud storage services
  • No external APIs (except optional OpenAI for AI summaries if you provide your own API key)

9. OPTIONAL AI FEATURES

ResearchMate includes an optional AI summarization feature:

  • Requires you to provide your own OpenAI API key
  • Your API key is stored locally in your browser
  • When you use AI summary, text is sent to OpenAI's servers
  • OpenAI's privacy policy applies to data sent to their API
  • This feature is completely optional and disabled by default

If you don't use the AI summary feature, no data ever leaves your device.

10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

ResearchMate is safe for users of all ages. Since we don't collect any personal information or data on servers, there are no special considerations for children's privacy.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of ResearchMate after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. DATA SECURITY

Your data's security depends on your device and browser security:

  • Chrome encrypts extension data stored locally
  • Data is protected by your device's security measures
  • Use Chrome sync (optional) to backup extension data to your Google account
  • Regular exports provide additional backup protection

13. YOUR RIGHTS

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Right to access: View all your data anytime in the extension
  • Right to export: Export all data in multiple formats
  • Right to delete: Delete any or all data instantly
  • Right to portability: Export and import data freely

14. LEGAL COMPLIANCE

ResearchMate complies with:

  • Chrome Web Store User Data Policy
  • Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) - No personal data collected
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) - No personal data sold or shared

15. NO DATA BREACHES

Since ResearchMate doesn't collect or store data on servers, there is no risk of:

  • Data breaches affecting your information
  • Unauthorized access to your highlights
  • Server hacks exposing your data

Your data's security is entirely within your control on your own device.
