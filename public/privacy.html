Privacy Policy for ResearchMate

Last Updated: January 2025

1. INTRODUCTION

This privacy policy explains how ResearchMate ("we", "our", "the extension") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Chrome extension.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

ResearchMate collects and stores:

  • Email address (for account authentication only)
  • Text highlights you save from webpages
  • Tags, notes, and metadata you add to your highlights
  • Source URLs and page titles of saved content

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information solely to:

  • Authenticate your account securely
  • Store and sync your highlights across your devices
  • Provide search and organization features
  • Enable export and citation generation

We do NOT:

  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Track your browsing history
  • Access webpages unless you actively save content
  • Monitor your online activity

4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase with:

  • Industry-standard encryption (both in transit and at rest)
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Data center redundancy for reliability

5. YOUR RIGHTS

You have complete control over your data. You can:

  • Access all your stored data at any time through the extension
  • Export your data in multiple formats (TXT, JSON, Markdown)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Request data deletion by contacting us

6. DATA DELETION

To delete your account and all data:

  • Contact us at [netnetku21@gmail.com]
  • We will permanently delete all your data within 30 days
  • Deletion is irreversible and complete

7. CHROME PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

ResearchMate requests these Chrome permissions for specific purposes:

  • storage - Store your highlights and settings locally for offline access
  • activeTab - Read selected text only when you click "Save to ResearchMate"
  • contextMenus - Add a convenient right-click menu option
  • scripting - Extract selected text and page metadata when you save
  • Access to all websites - Allow saving highlights from any site you choose to visit

Important: We only access webpages when you actively use the save feature. We do not track or monitor your browsing activity.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

ResearchMate is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the extension. Continued use of ResearchMate after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

ResearchMate uses Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Firebase's privacy policy applies to data stored on their servers. View Firebase's privacy policy at: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

11. LEGAL COMPLIANCE

ResearchMate complies with:

  • Chrome Web Store User Data Policy
  • Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) for EU users
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) for California users
